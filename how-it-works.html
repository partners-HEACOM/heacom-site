How it works

  1. Say hello. Send an enquiry or book a time. Tell us a little about your business or organisation.
  2. A 20-minute chat. We listen to your story and your goals. No preparation needed.
  3. Your 3 concepts. Within 7 days you get three Mandarin video concepts and one rough sample clip.
