A Sydney family business, powered by AI

HEACOM PTY LTD is run by Forrest Yin and Ying Fan, a family in Sydney.

We started HEACOM to help local businesses, councils and non-profits have a stronger, more meaningful connection with Mandarin-speaking communities. Not through volume posting, but through real stories told in natural Chinese.

[FOUNDER PHOTO]

Ying

Ying is a native Mandarin speaker and our storyteller. She writes the Chinese captions and voice, and she edits every piece of Chinese before it's published.

Forrest

Forrest is your main contact. He looks after clients, plans and the day-to-day running of the business.

Powered by AI, checked by people

AI helps us draft copy and translations, lay out designs, suggest replies and put together reports. That's how a small family team can work quickly. But nothing goes out on autopilot: Ying edits everything to native quality, and we post by hand on verified accounts.

What we stand for

  • Truth first. No invented clients, reviews or results, and no AI images passed off as real people.
  • Heartfelt, not high-volume. We'd rather tell one story well than post ten things nobody remembers.
  • Natural Chinese. It should read like a person talking, not a translation.
  • Small on purpose. You work directly with us.
