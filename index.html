Your story, told to Australia's Mandarin-speaking community

Across Australia, 685,274 people speak Mandarin at home.1 Many of them find restaurants, services and local news on WeChat, RedNote and TikTok/Douyin, not on the apps most Australian businesses use.

HEACOM helps you show up there as a familiar face, not just another ad. We find the real story behind your business, such as a family recipe, a founder's first shop or a moment from your community. Then we tell it in natural Chinese, in the formats these platforms are built for.

Why HEACOM

We're a Sydney family business. Ying is a native Mandarin storyteller who writes and edits every word of Chinese we publish. Forrest looks after our clients and keeps things running. AI does the heavy lifting on drafts, layouts and reports, so a small team can give you care and speed. A person checks everything before it goes out.

We care about heartfelt stories, not volume posting. We promise honest work and no fake reviews. We'll never promise you follower or sales numbers we can't stand behind.
