Four ways we can help

Pick one, or let us join them up. Every piece of Chinese we publish is written for Chinese readers first, never translated line by line.

(a) Mandarin social media management

We look after your Chinese social media accounts and take care of your marketing to the Mandarin-speaking community, from day-to-day posts to the conversations that follow.

  • Platforms: WeChat (Official Account and Channels), RedNote (小红书), TikTok/Douyin and others that suit your business, where the platform allows. Some apps limit who can open a business account. We'll tell you upfront what's possible for you.
  • Help setting up and verifying accounts. Any platform verification fees are passed on at cost.
  • A content plan, posts written in natural Simplified Chinese, and keywords for your suburb.
  • Replies to comments and messages in Mandarin.
  • A short report in English, so you always know what was posted and how it went.

(b) Heartfelt Chinese story videos, and any ad format

Short videos that people actually watch to the end. We find the story worth telling and bring it to life.

  • Anime-style illustrated stories, AI-assisted and directed and edited by us. They work well when you don't have much footage, and they're easy to remember.
  • Your own photos and footage, where you have them, cut together with captions and music.
  • Any ad format you need: short story videos, image posts, carousels, cover images and paid-ad versions.
  • Sized for each platform: 9:16 vertical video for TikTok/Douyin and WeChat Channels, and 3:4 images for RedNote.
  • Simplified Chinese captions, with an English translation so you know exactly what it says. [CONFIRM: English captions as standard]
  • Music chosen to suit your brand.

Filming: if you'd like on-site footage, talk to us and we'll work out what fits. [CONFIRM: filming scope]

(c) Brand ambassadors at Chinese cultural events

Some conversations are best had face to face. We provide friendly, bilingual (English and Mandarin) brand ambassadors who represent your real products at Chinese cultural events and shopping-centre pop-ups: Lunar New Year, Mid-Autumn and other community celebrations. [CONFIRM: Cantonese]

  • Online and offline, together: teaser posts on RedNote and WeChat before the event, then a recap video and a follow-up on enquiries afterwards.
  • Ambassadors briefed and supervised by HEACOM.
  • A simple enquiry form at the stall, with people's consent, and a lead report afterwards.
  • Our ambassadors welcome people, share factual information and collect enquiries. Where your product needs licensed advice, your own licensed team handles advice and sales.
  • Stall or site fees are quoted separately.

(d) Community engagement for councils and non-profits

Translated flyers matter, but they don't always reach people. We help councils and non-profits share important messages on the channels the community actually uses, in Chinese that sounds like a neighbour talking, not a notice on a noticeboard.

  • Community services: libraries, seniors' programs, playgroups, health and wellbeing.
  • Events and festivals, such as Lunar New Year and Mid-Autumn.
  • Public information: recycling and waste, safety, consultations and changes to services.
  • Non-profit programs: volunteering, carer support and in-language health messages.
  • Running Chinese social media channels for a campaign, where the platform allows, plus on-the-ground community engagement.

We agree the scope, timeline and approvals upfront. We work within procurement processes and can provide quotes, ABN details and documentation as needed.

What we need from you: a short chat about your story and goals; any photos or clips you already have (optional); quick feedback on drafts.

Paid advertising spend, if you choose to run ads, is billed separately by the platform.
